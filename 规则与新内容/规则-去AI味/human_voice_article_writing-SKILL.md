# Human Voice Article Writing

## Choose the structure from the story

- Start from the event or point the post needs to make and let that order set the structure.

- Vary the structure from one post to the next instead of reusing one fixed skeleton.

- Keep the voice, persona and series thread consistent while the structure follows the story.

- Keep lists and numbered headings when they fit the story and remove them when they sound forced.

## Put concrete detail and the ending in place

- Add at least one verifiable concrete detail such as a named step, a number from the work or a checkable result.

- Ask the user for a concrete detail when the brief has none and do not invent one to fill the gap.

- Put any follow request at the end of the post and keep a question ending when the post calls for one.

- Do not open with a follow request or a self introduction.

## Remove the signals that read as AI flavor

- Cut formulaic closers that warn the reader or restate the difficulty in polished words.

- Rewrite sentences that sound like a summary of the post instead of a person telling what happened.

- Match each image caption to the body flow while keeping the image role as cover, scene, key points or ending.

- Read the draft aloud in your head and rewrite any line you would not say to a friend.

## Check the draft before approval

- Check the opening, the concrete detail, the ending and every image caption against the rules above.

- Compare the draft with the previous post and change the structure when the two share the same skeleton.

- Show the user the exact final text and media for approval before any publish or schedule step.

- Record which concrete detail you used so a later correction can be traced to the draft.

## Alternatives for better results

- Ask the user for one real detail from the work before drafting when the brief is thin.

- Test a different opening line on the next post and compare comments and saves with the last post.

- Keep a short list of phrases the reader called out as AI flavor and check each new draft against it.
